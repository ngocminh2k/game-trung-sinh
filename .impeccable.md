# Design Context — Mực & Ngọc (Ink & Jade)

## Product

A PC-first, keyboard-first xianxia cultivation & reincarnation RPG for Vietnamese and English
speaking fans of the genre. The player is a reincarnated mortal with a **defective spirit root**
who must find their own crooked path to ascension. Tone: playful, warm, satisfying — never grimdark,
never bureaucratic.

## Audience

- Cultivation / xianxia / reincarnation fiction fans.
- Bilingual players: Vietnamese is the primary language, English is a first-class peer.
- Players who enjoy deterministic systems, light resource management, and cozy repetition.

## Aesthetic direction

- **Ink-wash and paper-and-jade**: rice-paper backgrounds, soft ink strokes, jade green accents,
  vermilion seals used sparingly as emphasis.
- Suggested palette anchors: rice paper `#f5efe0`, ink `#22211f`, jade `#3e8e6d`, vermilion `#b3402a`,
  muted gold `#c8a24b`.
- Typography: a serif display face for headings and story text; a humanist sans for UI labels.
- Feedback should feel tactile: brush-stroke transitions, gentle paper rustle energy, satisfying
  "stamp" moments on achievements and endings.
- Whimsical, not cartoonish. Elegant, not austere.

## Explicit prohibitions

- No dark neon dashboard look. No cyberpunk gradients, no glowing HUD chrome.
- No real-world nations, governments, or contemporary politics in content.
- No gore-heavy horror framing; danger exists but stays playful.

## Accessibility & input

- Keyboard-first: every interaction reachable without mouse; visible focus states.
- PC-first layouts (desktop window ≥ 1280px), graceful down to tablet.
- Both locales must stay in parity at all times (tests enforce this).
- Color is never the only carrier of meaning.

## System feel

- Deterministic engine underneath: same seed + same actions = same world.
- Free-text input is allowed but gently bounded: after repeated invalid input the game converges
  the player back onto suggested actions instead of dead-ending them.
- Danger is telegraphed with clear warnings before it lands.
