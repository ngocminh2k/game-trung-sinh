# Micro Game Design Document Template

## 1. Title & Elevator Pitch
- **Game Title:**  
- **One-Sentence Hook:**  
  - *What’s the core idea in one punchy sentence?*
  - *What makes this game irresistibly fun?*
  - *How does the title hint at a twist or subversion of typical tropes?*

## 2. High Concept & Theme
- **Core Concept:**  
  - *What makes your game unique?*
  - *What emotional response are you aiming for?*
  - *How can the theme drive unexpected gameplay interactions?*
- **Theme & Tone:**  
  - *What’s the overall vibe (quirky, dark, playful)?*
  - *Which classic tropes are you embracing or turning on their head?*
  - *How does the tone support rapid iteration and testing?*

## 3. Core Systems Overview
- **Main Systems:**  
  - List 2–3 primary systems (combat, resource management, exploration, etc.)
- **System Interactions:**  
  - *How do these systems connect or affect each other?*
  - *Which interactions could amplify the fun or create surprising combos?*
  - *Are there any "darlings" that might need trimming to keep the scope tight?*

## 4. Player Verbs & Engagement
- **Key Actions (Verbs):**  
  - List 3–4 core player actions (dash, shoot, craft, etc.)
- **Action Impact:**  
  - *How does each action drive progress or interact with systems?*
  - *Which actions feel the most satisfying, and why?*
  - *Can you prototype these actions independently to test their “feel”?*

## 5. Core Gameplay Loops
- **Primary Loop:**  
  - *Describe the repeatable cycle (explore → engage → upgrade → repeat)*
- **Feedback & Rewards:**  
  - *What immediate feedback/reward does each loop provide?*
  - *How does the loop continuously deliver fun and surprise?*
  - *What potential choke points might you need to cut or iterate on quickly?*

## 6. Mechanics & Features
- **Primary Mechanics:**  
  - *List the essential rules and interactions that support your core loop.*
- **Secondary/Bonus Mechanics:**  
  - *What extras enhance or spice up the experience (if time permits)?*
  - *Which mechanic poses the highest risk for complexity, and can it be simplified?*
  - *Can you prototype these mechanics separately with dedicated art or gameplay tests?*

## 7. Progression & Replayability
- **Progression Curve:**  
  - *How does the game escalate or change over time?*
- **Replay Loops:**  
  - *Any emergent gameplay or hidden combos encouraging repeated play?*
  - *How does progression amplify fun and novelty at every stage?*
  - *What unexpected elements can subvert the player’s expectations on replay?*

## 8. Visual & Audio Style
- **Art Direction:**  
  - *Minimalist, pixel art, vector, etc.?*
- **Sound Vibe:**  
  - *Chiptune, synth, orchestral?*
  - *How do visuals and audio reinforce core gameplay and fun moments?*
  - *Could rapid prototype tests with placeholder art help nail the mood?*
  - *What trope can you subvert with a twist in style?*

## 9. Scope & MVP Definition
- **Must-Have Features:**  
  - *What’s non-negotiable for a working build?*
- **Nice-to-Haves:**  
  - *What can be cut or postponed if time is tight?*
  - *Which features are crucial for delivering the core fun experience?*
  - *What can be quickly prototyped to validate your idea before full asset creation?*

## 10. Schedule & Milestones
- **Week 1:**  
  - *Concept finalization, core loop prototype, essential assets*
- **Week 2:**  
  - *Feature polish, playtesting, balancing, bug fixes*
- **Key Milestones:**  
  - *Identify checkpoints to ensure you’re on track.*
  - *What small, rapid prototypes can test key fun elements?*
  - *Are there slots for separate art and gameplay prototype iterations?*

## 11. Risks & Contingency Plans
- **Potential Roadblocks:**  
  - *Identify any “could break” areas in systems or scope*
- **Backup Ideas:**  
  - *Simpler alternatives if a system proves too heavy*
  - *Which features will you kill early if testing shows they aren't fun?*
  - *How can you test risky systems quickly to validate their contribution to overall fun?*

## 12. Final Pitch & Next Steps
- **One-Sentence Summary:**  
  - *Reframe your hook after planning – does it still pack a punch?*
- **Immediate Next Action:**  
  - *What’s the very next step to kickstart development?*
  - *How will you measure if the final concept delivers satisfying, engaging fun?*
  - *What micro-prototype can you build to validate your pitch before diving in?*
