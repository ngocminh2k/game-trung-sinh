# Simple Game Design Document Template

__GAME TITLE__

*Game Tagline*

*Game Credits \(basically, who made what\)*

__GAME IDEA__

- *What is your game about? What makes your game interesting, unique, special, or worth noticing/playing? Try to keep it concise\. The minimum would be a short phrase and the maximum would be 3 sentences\. Make sure to provide content warnings if needed\. \(Example: “An orange that becomes sentient tries to run away to avoid becoming juice” or “A long epic about becoming a true hero of the masses that combines rhythm, action, and puzzle game mechanics”\)*

__GAME OBJECTIVE__

__	__

__MAIN OBJECTIVE__

- 
	- *Why did you make this game? What is the game for or what is its intended purpose? What should the game be able to do? Only ONE main objective is recommended but you could also make up to THREE main objectives\. Objective/s can be about how you’re making the game for fun, learning, an advocacy, or something else entirely\. \(Example: “To make the player realise that short games can be fun too” or “To create a relatable experience that would help the player do more introspection”\) *

__GAMEPLAY GOAL/S__

- 
	- *What kind of gameplay do you want the player to experience? How do you want the player to feel when playing your game? Are you trying to make a hard and challenging game or are you making something more casual and relaxed? When the player is playing your game, how would you like them to react or how would you like them to describe it to their friends? \(Example: “To make the player feel compelled to get all the achievements” or “To let the player enjoy the game even after a lot of levels have passed”\)*

__PRESENTATION GOAL/S__

- 
	- *How do you want the player to feel about the assets they perceive in the game? Presentation can be any combination of the following: text \(dialogue, flavor text\), visual \(art, interface\), and audio \(music, sound design\)\. This is basically about how you want to present these things in your game and what kind of goals do you have for them\. \(Example: “To create an alluring but uncomfortable atmosphere” or “To make something nostalgic and light\-hearted that would force the player feel fluffy inside”\)*

__GENRE__

- *What kind of game are you making? What kind of genre do you think your game is based on its form, style, and subject matter? \(Example: “Singleplayer Dark\-fantasy RPG” or “Co\-op Rom\-Com Puzzle Sim” *

__TARGET AUDIENCE__

- *Who do you want to play the game? The game is aimed for what type of audience? You may include an age bracket, the gender/s, or the possible interests of the people you want to play your game\. \(Example: “LGBT\+ millenials who are suffering from a mid\-life crisis and loves 90s and early 2000s cartoons” or “9 to 16\-year\-old boys who like mysteries and pranks”\)*

__PLATFORM__

- *Where and/or how is your game going to be played? \(Example: “Offline Windows PC” or “Online – Internet Browser”\)*

__SUMMARY__

- *Elaborate on what your game is about\. This is why the “GAME IDEA” portion should be concise since you’d be able to tell more about your game in this part\. If your game has a story, give a nice summary here\. You may also add additional info like characters or flow of the game\.*

__GAMEPLAY__

__	__

__MECHANICS__

- 
	- *How is your game played? What actions should be done in order to progress through the game? Describe the mechanics involved in your game\. \(Example: “Collecting  cards to stack points” or “Selecting appropriate dialogue choices”\)  *

__	__

__FEATURES__

- 
	- *Game distinguishers\. What does your game have to offer? What do you want players to take note of in the game? A bullet\-type list for this is preferred\. \(Example: “Includes subtitles for different languages and complete English voice\-over” or “Colourblind\-friendly palette”\)*

__PRESENTATION __*\(It’s alright to omit parts that are not relevant to your game\.\)*

__	__

__TEXT	__

- 
	- *What tone do you want the text in your game to have? What style of formatting do you intend to use for the text in your game? “Text” in your game includes, but is not limited to, the following: story, dialogue, flavor text, and descriptions\. You may also cite relevant sources of inspiration\. \(Example: “Casual and more genuine tone in text that’s sprinkled with just the right amount of humor” or “Minimal and striaght to the point, almost apathetic or robotic in tone”\)*

__	__

__VISUAL__

- 
	- *What visual style do you want to use for your game? What kind of palette are you going to use? How much do you want to rely on visuals for your game? “Visuals” includes, but is not limited to, the art and interface present in your game\. Make sure to cite sources of inspiration as well\. Adding images to this part is highly recommended\. \(Example: “Painterly\-like art paired with a rigid interface that reflects the main character” or “Cute, colourful, and easy to digest or distinguish art inspired by mascots of franchises that children adore”\)*

__	__

__AUDIO__

- 
	- *What kind of music will be used in the game? What kind of techniques will you apply in the sound design of the game? How much do you intend to rely on the audio for your game? “Audio” is the music and sound design used in your game\. Inspirations  for your game’s audio should be cited as well\. Links would be nice to add in this part\. \(Example: “Soundtrack inspired by hardcore punk with a very energetic and dynamic sound design” or “Very minimal sound design that’s borderline creepy in execution”\)*

__DEVELOPMENT SCHEDULE__

- *How long do you think development would be? What is your target date for each milestone? When are you planning to launch the game? \(Example: “1\-3 months of development, targer launch: April 2021” or “2 years of development, demo launch: February 2023\)*

__POSSIBLE HURDLES__

- *What are the problems you think you might encounter during the production of your game? State the factors or things that might negatively affect the development of the game\. You may also write down possible solutions for the hurdles stated\. \(Example: “Limited time and budget” or “Insufficient research on subject matter might make the presentation bad”\)*
