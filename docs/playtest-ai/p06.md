- a83 — Sat back and did the math on the whole economy. Nowhere — market, merchant, inventory, system panel — can I buy low and sell high. There is no arb, no crafting, no loan, no conversion. Best loop = one blind +40/−20 daily spin. There is no lever.

## Fullerton 5 questions
- Q1 time pressure (deadline/night): None felt in 8 days. Rest ("Nghỉ") restores hp/qi fully and advances the day with zero cost; I never hit a hard clock, an overdue quest, or a danger I had to outpace. Low drive to economize.
- Q2 broken-root identity: The gating rule (spend stat points before you may cultivate) is the strongest "broken root must work for it" beat, but it's a friction prompt, not identity. I never risked death (hp never under 74), so the "weak cultivator" fantasy never had teeth.
- Q3 decision clarity — one clear + one unclear: CLEAR: "Nghỉ" reliably restores 100/60 and I understood it in one click. UNCLEAR: "Quay số" — its cost is invisible, its odds invisible, its result is delivered as a silent HUD number and can be LOSS. I could not make an informed decision ever.
- Q4 learning curve (slowest mechanic): The economy. It took me most of the session to even discover that the only revenue event (Quay số) exists and is one-per-day, and I still never found a sell or convert. The systems give no affordance that a trade economy is there.
- Q5 narrative engagement (NPC/beat that stayed, reactivity): Bà đồng Liên's dialog is the only text that changed my numbers visibly (+40 earlier), and Merchant Bảo's flavor is pleasant — but nothing reacted to my playstyle or money. No NPC commented on my riches or my gambling. The market feels static.
