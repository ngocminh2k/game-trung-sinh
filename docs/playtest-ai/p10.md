- persona: Casual phone-games player. English UI, STORY difficulty. Never reads a manual, clicks the biggest-looking button, ignores anything requiring typing, doesn't know what "qi" is and won't look it up, closes text-heavy screens. Report = onboarding audit.
- seed/session: p10 / ink-and-jade (server-picked from p10-seed) / story / en

- path: Greenwood Village → Cloudgather Market (day 1–4) → back to Greenwood Village

## Moments (timestamped by my own action count)

Clicked it — the chrome flipped to English. **First lesson: the localization switch is invisible to an actual player.** A real casual player never gets past this screen.
- a8–a12 — Character creation: ten "Systems" as cards. I couldn't parse any of them ("phế căn"? "qi"?), so I did what my persona always does: picked the one whose English words I recognized — the Merchant ("money" — obviously good). Chose Story difficulty from a visible picker. Zero explanation of what a System *does*; I'm trusting vibes.

I'm a Socializer player and the entire social layer of this game just vanished. Nothing pulled me back; I kept playing only because the moderator budget says so.

## Fullerton 5 questions

- **Q1 time pressure (deadline/night):** Effectively zero experienced. At STORY difficulty nothing ever chased me: `corrections: 0`, `nightForgotten: false` across 4 days. The day counter ticks only when *I* press Rest. No timer, no consequence, no tension — the "forgotten night" mechanic never announced itself to me at all. Fine for this persona, but I never learned the clock exists.
- **Q2 broken-root identity:** Never felt it. "Phế căn" (waste spirit root) is explained nowhere in reach of an EN player — the journal is unclickable (topbar bug), dialogue is VI-only, and my stage sat at 0 the whole run so the "trash root that can't cultivate" fantasy had no mechanical expression. I allocated attribute points blind into FORTUNE; nothing about that screamed *broken root*. The merchant System gave me exactly one identity moment: Gacha gold. 
- **Q3 decision clarity:** Clearest one example: **map pins with "(N steps)"** — I always knew what a click cost and where it led, and it worked every time. Ugliest unclear example: **the forced stat allocation** — "Allocate 2 points before continuing" with four undefined stats, no preview of effect, no cancel; my merchant = FORTUNE logic was pure guesswork, and Train beforehand had hidden hp/qi costs I only discovered by paying them.
- **Q4 learning curve (slowest mechanic):** Gacha. It took 6 attempts across 3 days and one gold *loss* to induce "once per day at market, random ± result, silent." Every data point arrived as either nothing or a raw number delta in the HUD. Cultivation (Train/Stage/qi) I never learned at all — Train looked like it did nothing except hurt me, so a manual-ignoring player stops pressing it by day 2. Night/corrections never surfaced.
- **Q5 narrative engagement:** None reachable. Three NPCs talked to (Bao, Lien, Phung) — all modals Vietnamese in EN locale, all choices echoed verbatim as the PC's reply, all NPC responses truncated in the digest. No event prose fired on any of my 46 registered actions; travel produced no scene text. The story this game is named after left zero footprint on my run. As a Socializer, my whole genre is dialogue, and the dialogue is unlocalized — that's not friction, that's a wall.

## Bugs & friction

- [BLOCKER-onboarding] NPC dialogue (options, speaker lines, "Đóng") is Vietnamese even with locale=en (a13–16, a31, a51–52). Affects the game's core social loop; it was my uninstall moment.
- [BUG] "Open Journey journal" is permanently unclickable — Playwright times out with `proto-topbar … intercepts pointer events` (a27–28, reproduced twice with fresh indices; button also at a46/a51). Journal/exposition completely unreachable.
- [BUG] Dialogue echo: the chosen option text is re-printed verbatim as Lâm Phàm's reply (Merchant Bao, Fortune Lien, Cook Phung — every talk), and NPC responses are truncated in HỘP THOẠI ("Đầ…").
- [BUG] Gacha (draw_lottery) is dead in the village (2 silent no-ops, not even telemetry) and in the market it fires silently: +40g day 1, −20g day 3, then exact repeats of the click do nothing with no "already today" message (a18–19, a24, a54–55).
- [CONFUSION] Train deducts hp −6 and qi −10 per press with zero event feedback, stage unchanged (0 after two trains), then hard-locks the turn with an unexplained stat allocation (a41–42).
- [CONFUSION] "Try" (a38) and "Gather" (a47) and the Fortune-stall pin (a49) are silent no-ops — indistinguishable from broken buttons.
- [CONFUSION] "Move" is a cursor-mode toggle hiding behind an action-verb label; the VI placeholder `<nhập>` in the English Command field is the only untranslated chrome outside dialogue.
- [NIT] Hidden EN switch on the VI-only main menu (found only via the API's unfiltered listing) — first-impression blocker for any EN player.
- [NIT] Screenshots are visually near-empty: locations render as flat beige fields with a small text panel (p10-23, p10-34, p10-46); only the dialogue modal has real visual structure — and it's the VI part.
- [NIT] HUD mixes emoji/CJK glyphs (人 in the title bar; VI/EN buttons) that never appear in the accessible control listing, so they're pure decoration or pure noise depending on who you are.

## Verdict

- clarity 2/10 — Rest and map pins are honestly great; every other system (qi, stage, Train, Gacha, Systems, night) is silent or Vietnamese.
- agency 3/10 — I could always click something, but the world either refused silently or asked me to spend unexplained points; nothing I did mattered visibly.
- readability of HUD 4/10 — the English HUD numbers are fine; the emoji/CJK garnish isn't in the a11y tree, the event log is VI-only and truncated, and there's no scene to read visually.
- would-play-again 2/10 — the one thing that pulled me forward was a mystery button that handed me free gold once per day; everything else I came back for — people, story, my character's identity — was behind a Vietnamese modal or an unclickable header.
