- Q2 broken-root identity — Never surfaced to me. I never stopped pushing the quest to learn who my
  broken-root character is; the game never made me. No identity moment landed in 3 days.

  cost on every pin). UNCLEAR: how to reach stage 2 — I trained and dumped 2 stat points across
  SPIRIT and BODY and `stage` never moved, and the journal I'd use to re-read the objective is
  unclickable.

  fight, gather, allocate, rest) visibly touches `stage`, yet the covenant gates on it. I burned ~8
  actions on train/allocate across both days before concluding there's no demonstrated path.

  and Village NPCs were pure accept-quest noise I skipped. The ONE thing that hooked me — the
  covenant objective "Reach stage 2" — was also the one thing that refused to resolve. Engagement =
  a promise with no payoff; that's a tease, not a story.
