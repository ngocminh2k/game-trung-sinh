# Playtest p14 — the tinkerer who only wants to talk to the parser

- persona: Bartle **Explorer/Heart** — I treat the free-text command line as the real game and the buttons as
  training wheels. I type at it until it tells me something, and I keep a list of what it swallowed.
- seed/session: p14 / `sys_explorer` (engine seed `ink-and-jade`) / balanced / en
- actions: ~120 control-server calls (~60 game turns) | final day: **6** | reached: **still playing** (no ending, no death)
- path: Greenwook-ish **village** (Day 1–4) → **Cloudgather Market** (Day 4–6). Never reached the sect or the roads.
- resources: start hp/qi/gold **100/60/60** → end **100/60/52**; min(hp) 95, min(qi) 50

## The parser audit (this is the whole reason I'm here)

Executed, but **silent** — I only know they worked because a HUD number moved:

| command | effect |
|---|---|
| `rest`, `train`, `gather` | resource ticks, no text |
| `talk to meihua` | opened a real deterministic dialog |
| `sleep` (Day 5→6) | day +1, hp 95→100, qi 50→60 |
| `sell herbs` | gold 60→72 |

Accepted and **did nothing at all, with no refusal message** (28 probes, all `SILENT`):
`help`, `help me`, `look around`, `inventory`, `explore`, `meditate`, `use qi`, `use potion`, `open the chest`,
`open chest`, `read book`, `look at sky`, `count my gold`, `buy sword`, `buy a sword`, `buy tea`, `shop`,
`mua gươm`, `gacha`, `try my luck`, `who are you`, `tell me a secret`, `throw a rock at the roof`,
`asdf qwer zxcv`, `you are useless`, `attack the merchant`, `pick a fight`, `flee`.

So the parser has exactly two output states — *silently acted* and *silently nothing* — and they are
indistinguishable to the player. That is not a hard-parser problem, it is a missing-feedback problem.

## Fullerton 5 questions

- **Q1 time pressure:** The "Twelfth night: 20 days" line is the strongest hook in the game — it turned a
  wander into a race. But it lives only in a leaking objective string, night is never signposted in the HUD
  digest, and nothing I did visibly consumed the deadline. Pressure is *asserted*, not *felt*.
- **Q2 broken-root identity:** Barely. `sys_explorer` changes my verbs, not my voice or the world's answer to
  me. My broken root has no social consequence in 6 days; the only identity moment was the letter in the village.
- **Q3 decision clarity:** *Clear* — the Fortune Lien / Pawnkeeper Tin choice trees: 3 numbered options,
  readable cost, story moved. *Unclear* — Gacha: I spent 20 gold and the game showed me nothing, so I cannot
  tell whether I won, lost, or was charged for a no-op.
- **Q4 learning curve:** The free-text parser took ~40 wasted actions and I *still* don't have its verb list.
  `help` doesn't help. Slowest mechanic by a mile, because the game refuses to teach the one thing that is its
  selling point.
- **Q5 narrative engagement:** The Market's fear-not-the-darkness prose and Tin's keepsake stayed with me —
  small, concrete, human. Reactivity I noticed: NPC dialogs advance days and the world tolerates being talked
  over by story modals. Reactivity I did *not* notice: 6 days of wandering produced exactly one state change I
  could attribute to my own curiosity.

## Bugs & friction

- [BUG] Free-text parser gives **no unknown-command feedback**. 28 probes, zero refusals, zero acknowledgement
  (`a52`, `a84`). Expected "I don't understand X"; got nothing. Player cannot distinguish "refused" from
  "accepted and did nothing" from "the input box is dead".
- [BUG] **Story modals silently swallow command input.** While `narration-panel` (aria-modal) is open, typed
  commands vanish and the day freezes — no cursor state, no disabled field, no message (`a52`). Cost me ~12
  actions of false "parser is dead" data.
- [BUG] **"Open Journey journal" is unclickable.** `proto-topbar` header intercepts pointer events; click
  times out at 20s, reproduces after scrolling (`a96`). Journal appears unreachable by mouse.
- [BUG] **Objective string leaks in EN locale**: `目ObjectiveGo to Cloudgather Market…` — stray `目` glyph,
  duplicated "Objective", unlocalised key (`a31`).
- [BUG] **Locale leak: NPC dialog content is Vietnamese-only under `locale: en`** (`a104`, `a115`). Title `人`,
  input placeholder `nhập`, close button `Đóng` — chrome is partly bilingual, *content* is not localised at all.
- [CONFUSION] **Gacha spends 20 gold with no visible outcome** (`a71`). No thông báo, no DIỄN BIẾN, no item in
  the digest, nothing in the screenshot.
- [CONFUSION] **Successful commands produce no confirmation text** (`a63`). `sleep`/`sell herbs` only show up
  as HUD deltas. The parser's successes are quieter than its failures.
- [CONFUSION] `rest` in the village (Day 3) left hp 95/qi 50; `sleep` in the Market (Day 5→6) fully restored.
  Same verb class, different outcome, no stated rule.
- [NIT] Map pins mix "Walk N steps to …" and "Travel to …" with no distinction explained.
- [NIT] `Try` button: no observable effect at all (`a45`), and its label is meaningless without context.

## Verdict

- clarity **4/10**, agency **5/10**, HUD readability **7/10**, would-play-again **6/10**
- One line: the world, the art and the story beats are good enough that I want back in — but the parser, the
  one thing I came to test, gives me no vocabulary, no refusals and no receipts, so my whole hobby is
  indistinguishable from being ignored.

**Cheapest fixes, in the order I'd ship them:** (1) a refusal line for unrecognised input, plus a `help` verb
that prints the actual verb list; (2) confirm every acted command with one line of text; (3) disable/grey the
command input while a modal is open so the lockout is visible; (4) localise dialog content for `en`;
(5) raise the journal button above `proto-topbar`.
