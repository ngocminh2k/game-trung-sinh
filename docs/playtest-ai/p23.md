## The one question I was here to answer
> Ticket C2-01 — Quest Marker / Objective Line Stuck: Does the HUD "Mục tiêu" line or quest marker stay stuck after quest progression/turn-in, and how does the game guide casual players who wander off?

**Answer:** 
The objective line accurately retained the pending quest objective (`Nói chuyện với cụ Mai Hoa về lá thư.`) during the entire multi-location detour between Village and Market. However, attempting to turn in the quest revealed a critical UI blocker: opening the dialogue modal (`NpcChatModal`) triggers `onModalChange(true)`, which incorrectly flags the parent container (`proto-shell-wrap`) with `inert` and `pointer-events: none`. This locks up all dialogue choice buttons, preventing the turn-in from completing via click.

## Bugs & friction

- **[BLOCKER] Dialogue choices inert / non-clickable in NpcChatModal (a31–a34).** When `NpcChatModal` opens, `isDialogModalActive` triggers `onModalChange(true)`. In `src/ui/GameScreen.tsx`, `protoModalActive` applies `inert` and `pointer-events: none` to `.proto-shell-wrap`. Since `NpcChatModal` is rendered inside `ProtoShell` within `.proto-shell-wrap`, the modal makes itself inert and pointer-inert, rendering `[1] Hỏi về lá thư` and `[2] Rời đi` completely unclickable.
- **[CONFUSION] Redundant move steps emitted during zone travel.** Walking between Village and Market emitted 3 duplicate `move` steps at the same timestamp in telemetry.
