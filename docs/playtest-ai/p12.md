# Playtest p12 — the lonely reader who just wants the villagers to like them

- persona: Bartle **Club/Socializer**. A soft-hearted player who wants to *feel* affection
  numbers move. I give gifts, say nice things, and worry when someone looks sad. I care
  about being liked far more than being strong.
- seed/session: p12 / `ink-and-jade` / **story** difficulty / **EN** locale
- actions: **94** (server-counted)  |  final day: **4**  |  reached: **still playing — HARD-LOCKED**
  (game frozen on an undismissible "Allocate 2 points before continuing" modal; no ending, no death)
- path: village → market → village → thousand_herbs_valley (Cloudgather Market, Village Square,
  Thousand Herbs Valley)
- resources: start hp/qi/gold = **100 / 60 / 60** → end = **94 / 50 / 46**

## The one question I was here to answer
> Does the game ever show that I MATTERED to an NPC, or do NPCs stay flat no matter how kind I am?

**Answer: NPCs stay flat. Kindness does not accumulate, is not remembered, and — worst —
is never even displayed.** Affection is the one number this game refuses to let me see.

## Fullerton 5 questions

- **Q1 — time pressure (deadline/night):** Gentle. Story difficulty + the Rest day-cycle gave
  no real dread; I never felt the night "catching" me (telemetry `nightForgotten:false`).
  For a Socializer that's fine — I didn't want stress, I wanted people. The *only* pressure I
  felt was the allocation modal screaming "before continuing."
- **Q2 — broken-root identity:** The Scholar + frail-start reads clearly and warmly. hp/qi are
  low, the prose is self-aware about weakness. The identity lands. What *doesn't* land is that
  a broken-root wanderer could still be *loved* here — the social half of my identity has no
  mechanic to express itself.
- **Q3 — decision clarity:**
  - *Clear example:* Buy in the market — cost visible, gold drops 60→46, unambiguous.
  - *Unclear example:* the "Allocate 2 points" modal demands a decision but exposes no
    control to make it. The clearest instruction in the whole game ("do this before
    continuing") is the one thing I literally cannot execute.
- **Q4 — learning curve (slowest mechanic):** **Affection/relationships** never learned,
  because nothing ever surfaces it. I understood train/gather/rest in ~15 actions; I understood
  *zero* of the social layer in 94. Gifting is the intended verb for my persona and it doesn't exist.
- **Q5 — narrative engagement (reactivity I noticed):** There *is* day-to-day topic variation
  (day-2 lines differed from day-1), and dialog trees offer kind/closing options, so on the
  surface it feels alive. But none of it persists: Lan's warmest line repeats verbatim across
  days, and no choice changes anyone's displayed state. The warmth is **decorative, not reactive.**
  The beat that "stayed" with me was the sadness of the em-dash.

## Bugs & friction

- **[BLOCKER] Allocation modal soft-lock (a66→end).** After Train, game requires "Allocate 2
  points before continuing" but renders **no allocation UI** in the accessible control list
  (confirmed via `state?all=1`, 26/26 controls, screenshot). Every input refused. Progression
  dead-ends permanently; the only way out would be editing saved state, which the brief forbids.
  *This is where my run ended.* Suspect the same overlay/hit-test issue hides the point buttons.
- **[BLOCKER] `grid-overlay` / `proto-topbar` intercept pointer events → many controls
  unclickable.** `elementHandle.click: Timeout 20000ms` on **"Open Journey journal" (a31)**,
  the people/♥ tab, and finally the **"Menu" button (a94)**. The whole top bar and the NPC
  journal are unreachable for the entire session. I could never view affection, logs, or exit
  to menu. Likely the *root cause* of the allocation lock too (modal buttons sit under the overlay).
- **[BUG] Affection never displays numerically.** All NPC chips read **"♥ —"** permanently,
  across 4 days and many kind interactions. If an affection value exists in the engine, the UI
  hides it; if it doesn't, the ♥ glyph is a false promise. Either way the player gets no feedback.
- **[BUG] NPC dialog is a canned loop, not memory.** Herbalist Lan's reciprocation line was
  byte-identical on day 3 and day 4. Earlier, Ngo and Innkeeper Hanh shared identical lines
  too. Kind choices don't change what NPCs say.
- **[CONFUSION] No gifting / social verb.** Free-text *"give spirit herb to Meihua"* silently
  ignored; inventory items only "Use" (on self). A Socializer persona has no supported way to
  act on its central motivation.
- **[CONFUSION] EN locale is skin-deep.** UI chrome is English, but **all NPC dialog stays in
  Vietnamese.** The persona meant to read warmly can't parse the emotional payload.
- **[NIT] Repeated `move` steps emitted for one traversal** (telemetry shows 8+ `move` rows at
  the same timestamp, e.g. 7 village-moves in one step) — noisy, suggests a walk-path firing per
  tile rather than per destination.

## Verdict
- **clarity: 4/10** — the world is legible until you want anything social or you have to spend a
  point; then it goes silent.
- **agency: 2/10** — I could gather/train/rest/move, but I could not *gift*, could not *open the
  journal*, and finally could not *continue at all*. My persona's entire goal was unsupported.
- **readability of HUD: 5/10** — day/hp/qi/gold are crisp and pretty; the ♥ "—" affection slot is
  unreadable *by design*, which is the exact field I needed.
- **would-play-again: 1/10** — not because it's boring (the prose charmed me) but because I *cannot*
  play again on this save: day 4 is a permanent dead-end, and the fix lives behind a forbidden state edit.
- **one line why:** *It's a lovely, lonely little village that will not let me be kind to anyone,
  will not show me if I mattered, and then locks its one door with no handle on the inside.*

## For the analysis pass (the finding, stated plainly)
The social/affection layer is **invisible and non-persistent**, and there is a **progression
soft-lock** with no exposed remedy. As a Club-type player my single reason to keep clicking —
"did they come to like me?" — is never answered, and at day 4 the game asks me to make a choice it
has not given me a control to make. If NPCs had even *once* shown a ♥ number move after my
kindness, this report would read very differently. They never did.
