## Moments (timestamped by my own action count)

- a1–a5 — Opened the game, HUD readable at a glance (day/location/hp/qi/gold/stage). Chose the lake road away from the village immediately. First thing I noticed: travel between village *sub-locations* updates the map pins but never the HUD location name — the HUD says "Làng Thanh Mộc" whether I'm at the well or the bamboo rampart.
- a8–a12 — Clicked **Hái thảo** at the lakeshore. Nothing. Clicked again. Nothing. No message, no DIỄN BIẾN line, no stat change. Expected forage; the world simply refused me silently. (This became the running frustration of the session.)
- a13 — Typed free-text "khám phá" into the command input and hit Enter. Silently swallowed — no acknowledge, no rejection message. A text box that eats input with no feedback is worse than no text box.
- a16 — An unlabeled button landed me on `kind:"accept_quest"` in telemetry. I never saw a quest title, objective, or reward. I am a persona who refuses plots; the game took a quest from me while I watched. The UI never told me the plot had begun.
- a20 — **Tu luyện** in the village: no narration, but hp 100→95, qi 60→50 in the HUD. The only *visible* cost system in the game, and it is invisible. I learned what it does by reading numbers, not by reading text.
- a22 — **Nghỉ** = the only day-mover in the entire game as far as I could tell. Day 1→2. Nothing else advanced time; with no deadlines firing, the clock is decorative.
- a27 — Back at the village, Hái thảo again: silent no-op (2nd confirmation). Then market: bought a Tụ Khí Đan for 14g — but the **inventory panel is unreachable** (see Bugs), so I have never once seen the pill I own.
- a33–a35 — **Quay số** (lottery) at the market: gold 46→26 across two draws. The *result* of each draw appears nowhere in the text digest — only a screenshot shows it, in what looked like CJK glyphs. I spent 20 gold on a slot machine whose output the UI doesn't render. **Thử Vận** on top of that: also a full silent no-op (market and village).
- a44 — Travel pin to the sect (Sơn môn Vân Ẩn): the **only** location in the game whose travel actually changes the HUD location name. Arrival added one button the rest of the world never has: **"⚔ Giao chiến: Đệ Tử Mới"**. As a wanderer who refused every plot, I reached day 2 of a combat-capable roguelike and combat had never once been on offer. The sandbox doesn't fight you for refusing the plot — it just deletes the genre.
- a49 — Talked to Luyện dược Sâm (sect pill-refiner, Bạch Lạc Vi). She offered a mid-grade pill "nếu ngươi cần thì để giá h…" — the dialogue box **clips mid-sentence**; I had to re-GET /state to read the full line (the ✕-prefixed full text exists server-side; the card just truncates). I declined and walked out. She seemed fine with it. Nothing in the world changed because I declined.
- a57 — Closed my second story hook (Ngo kể chuyện, who dangled exactly my persona's mirror: "chuyện một vị tiền bối mang linh căn phế vẫn phi thăng"). Same result: refusal costs nothing, changes nothing.
- a60 — Typed "đứng nhìn mây trôi" at the sect gate. Third confirmed free-text silent no-op. Then clicked **"Đi tới Bậc đá xuống núi (2 bước)"** — and was teleported directly to the *village hub*, not to any descent node. A pin labeled "2 bước" that is instant, landing at a different place than its name implies.
- a62 — Hái thảo at the village hub proper: 4th silent no-op. Gave up. Screenshot p16-60.png: still the same flat button-wall as every other location — the sect has zero visual identity beyond the sword glyph on one button.

## Fullerton 5 questions

- **Q1 time pressure (deadline/night):** Effectively zero. Nghỉ is the only clock-tick; in two days of wandering no deadline, no night event, no expiry fired. `nightForgotten:false` in telemetry — I never even met the night system. The sandbox has a calendar, not a clock.
- **Q2 broken-root identity:** Almost absent from moment-to-moment play. The only mechanical taste: Tu luyện costs hp/qi that a "normal" cultivator presumably wouldn't bleed, and stage stayed 0 through everything. But no text ever said "your broken root does X." The identity is in the title, not the turn loop.
- **Q3 decision clarity:** Clearest: the sect pill offer — price, item, yes/no, all legible (once I un-clipped the text). Unclear: the unlabeled `accept_quest` button — I accepted a plot without knowing it was a plot; and the "Bậc đá xuống núi (2 bước)" pin which was secretly an instant village teleport.
- **Q4 learning curve (slowest mechanic):** Hái thảo, by a mile. Four clicks across three locations before I accepted the button is not broken-but-scheduled, just *absent where the game won't say so*. Runner-up: Quay số — I could not learn what it gives you because the result never enters the text feed.
- **Q5 narrative engagement:** Ngo the storyteller stayed with me — offering a broken-root ascension tale to a broken-root wanderer was the one beat that felt addressed to *me*. Reactivity to refusal: none measurable. Walk away from every hook and the game doesn't punish, doesn't comment, doesn't adapt. It goes quiet. The sandbox is deep enough to refuse, shallow enough that refusing is free and therefore weightless.

## Verdict

- clarity **4/10** — HUD numbers are clean, but four action buttons are voids that eat input without a word.
- agency **3/10** — I could walk anywhere, but almost nothing I did changed anything; the one real choice (accept/refuse plot) is consequence-free.
- readability of HUD **7/10** — dense, legible, mono-spaced, always current… except the location field, which lies about sub-locations.
- would-play-again **5/10** — the map *wants* to be wandered and the sect reveal is genuinely nice; but a roguelike where gathering, luck, free text, and inventory are all unreachable or silent isn't a sandbox yet, it's a lobby.
