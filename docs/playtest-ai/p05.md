- a97 — Map-seller: "Kẻ ở lại thì ít, người ra đi thì nhiều…" — a line already spoken by two other NPCs in two other towns. The writing is good writing; the reuse makes it feel like a stock deck.

Free content every day, which is generous, but the game never tells me a topic set is daily-refreshed, so I did not know whether I was seeing new material or re-listening to old.
- a105 — Final drawer read: `Người ở đây: 3` here vs 11 at the market — so it is a **per-location** roster, not a world count, and it has no total. `Biên niên: 25` (was 16) grew purely from talking. `Túi đồ & kho: 0` with no capacity. That last one is my bug list: I gathered and I was greeted and my bag is still empty.

## Fullerton 5 questions
- **Q1 time pressure (deadline/night): effectively none.** Nothing in the HUD warned of a deadline, and I only advanced a day by explicitly resting. Step costs are the only clock, and they are opaque: "Quầy quay vận mệnh" cost 3 steps when I first saw it and 8 steps later, with no explanation. Dynamic pricing without a visible rule reads as a bug, not as tension.
- **Q2 broken-root identity: this is the game's best asset and it is delivered through other people's eyes.** The whole world is a mirror that refuses to look away: the shrine, the card-seller and the scholar all frame the废根 as *something done to you by gazes*, not a stat. The scholar system reframing it as "linh căn khác biệt" was the sharpest beat — the game's own mechanics lie to you politely.
- **Q3 decision clarity.** Clear: travel. Pin labels carry the step cost in the button text, so route planning is a real, readable choice. Unclear: everything that isn't movement. Accepting quests, spinning the wheel, and "Thử Vận" all return no result, so a completionist cannot tell done from not-done and cannot plan around it.
- **Q4 learning curve.** Slowest system by far is the collection drawer. Once I found it (via the settings gear, not the intended button, which is covered), the tabs are useful but the semantics are unexplained: which counters are local (`Người ở đây: 3`), which are world totals (`1/13`), which are unbounded (`Biên niên: 25`), which are meaningless (`0`)? A completionist needs the denominator and the game withholds exactly one of them.
- **Q5 narrative engagement.** The beekeeper stayed with me: "Ong linh không bao giờ chích ngươi hai lần — lần đầu là lời cảnh báo, lần sau là bản án." Also the tailor's night event (animals standing perfectly still), and the auctioneer on "a technique whose owners had to forget its origin." Reactivity I noticed and liked: the chronicle counts conversations, and NPC topic pools refresh daily. Reactivity I needed and didn't get: nothing in the world acknowledged that I greeted 14 people and accepted 3 quests. Zero visible consequence for the most completionist run possible.

## Bugs & friction
- [BLOCKER] The `VI/EN` language toggle and the "Mở Hành trang và giang hồ" drawer button in the world view are both unclickable — Playwright reports the click intercepted by `<header data-od-id="topbar" class="proto-topbar">`. The topbar overlays them. Two functions are only reachable via the gear icon and the header tabs; the shortcut buttons are dead. (a2, a46, repeated)
- [BUG] Quest acceptance gives zero feedback. Three clicks on "Nhận nhiệm vụ" (a40–a45) → no toast, no log line, "Nhiệm vụ: 0/210" never moves, buttons persist. Expected "Đã nhận: <quest>". Telemetry shows 3× `accept_quest` landed, so the world heard me and the UI didn't.
- [BUG] Gambling is silent: "Thử Vận" (a99) and "Quay số" (a100) produced no result, no gold change, no log — though telemetry recorded `draw_lottery`. Same class as the `gather` that also showed nothing (a85).
- [BUG] NPC reply text is hard-truncated in the `HỘP THOẠI` digest at ~80 chars (seen at a93, a97, a103). Reading a full line from the text feed is impossible; only the screenshot carries it. For an API-driven read, that's data loss.
- [BUG] Chained clicks during the dialog open/close animation get intercepted by `proto-modal-backdrop .floor`, `.chat-stage`, or `.face`. One action per turn is the only reliable cadence; expect wasted actions if you batch.
- [CONFUSION] "Người ở đây: N" has no denominator and its scope silently changes per location (11 → 3). Cannot tell whether 3 means "3 in this valley" or "3 total". Completionist progress feedback: partial.
- [CONFUSION] Map step costs change between visits (3 → 8 for the fortune wheel) with no visible rule or notification.
- [CONFUSION] Day-2 topic refresh is unlabelled, so I could not tell new content from repeats.
- [NIT] At least three NPC lines are cross-reused: the "chạy nhanh nhất … nghĩa địa" line (Thợ rèn Đức / Đấu giá sư Hoàn), the "Kẻ ở lại thì ít…" line (Phiêu kiếm Phong / Chủ cầm đồ Tín / Bán bản đồ Mẫn), and the "Mỏng hơn trước…" linh-khí line (Học giả Minh / Người giữ vườn Viên), plus shared openers "Ừ, cái dáng của ngươi là biết vừa đi xa".
- [NIT] The event log on the right never updated during any of my actions, which is why silent actions read as failures rather than as gains.
