## Fullerton 5 questions

- **Q1 time pressure**: The `Day 2` counter and the "twelfth night" mention in the main objective are visible but soft — an EXPLORER can ignore them for long stretches and nothing bad happened on day 2. Good for wandering, low anxiety.
- **Q2 broken-root identity**: Starting stats (100 hp / 60 qi) and Elder Meihua's dialog paint the phế căn (broken-root) underdog clearly. The tonic quest reinforced it: even an elder needs this nobody's help gathering herbs.
- **Q3 decision clarity**: Clear — "Gather 3 spirit herbs." and "Bring the herbs to Meihua." were one-liners I never had to re-read. Unclear — the main line "Reach Storyteller teahouse, marked in vermilion..." was visually truncated ("opens your n") and I never found the teahouse as a reachable map pin; the lead eventually appeared as an NPC in the village instead. I could not tell what to do with it.
- **Q4 learning curve**: Slowest mechanic to learn was that turn-ins live in the Journey Journal → Quests tab, NOT in the NPC dialog. I instinctively clicked "Talk to Elder Meihua" first and hit an unclickable dialog modal (0/0 controls) before learning to use the journal.
- **Q5 narrative engagement**: Elder Meihua stuck with me — the same quest giver for both a main-line letter and a personal tonic. The Storyteller teahouse lead ("marked in vermilion") is a strong hook; I'd follow it next run.
