## Fullerton 5 questions
- Q1 time pressure (deadline/night): Under a day counter (day 1 → 2), but nothing made me *feel* a countdown for a churn player. I left for 3 real minutes and the game could not tell me what was urgent.
- Q2 broken-root identity: Remains thematic (Phế Căn Ký = Tale of the Broken Root) but invisible to a distracted me — I could not recall or re-find any defining trait; nothing re-anchored me.
- Q3 decision clarity — ONE CLEAR: the map pins with step counts ("Đi tới Cổng chợ Vân Tập (3 bước)") are excellent — I always knew distance. ONE UNCLEAR: the Journal/menu affordances that are rendered but unclickable — dead UI is worse than absent UI.
- Q4 learning curve (slowest mechanic): Re-orientation. Everything else (walk/fight/rest) I got in one touch. Understanding *where I am and what I was doing* never resolved — there is no such view.
- Q5 narrative engagement (NPC/beat that stayed): Rest as a beat landed (full heal + day tick = progress I felt). Conversations (elder, innkeeper, storyteller) gave color but nothing I could carry away and use after a gap.

## Bugs & friction
- [BLOCKER] Session DELETE destroys the entire run's save data. Load Game shows 5 empty slots after any reload. A player unable to continue is the highest-severity failure for this persona.
- [BUG] Journal "Open Journey journal" button (idx 46) → `elementHandle.click: Timeout 20000ms ... <header data-od-id="topbar" ...> intercepts pointer events`. Rendered but unclickable.
- [BUG] "Về menu" exit (`#game-exit-menu`, idx 47) → same click timeout. Cannot cleanly exit to menu; only kill the window.
- [BUG] "Skip to map" link (idx 0) → "element is outside of the viewport". Broken navigation shortcut.
- [BUG] English locale does not persist across reload; reverts to Vietnamese; EN toggle only visible in unfiltered control list.
- [CONFUSION] "Skip to map / Skip to inventory" links duplicate topbar buttons — two competing navigation idioms.

## Verdict
- clarity 5/10 — pins and Rest are clear, but there is no "your state right now" view for a returning player.
- agency 6/10 — I could act, but three key buttons (journal, exit, skip-map) are dead.
- readability of HUD 8/10 — day/location/hp/qi/gold read at a glance; the best part of the game.
- would-play-again 3/10 — the core loop is inviting, but it actively resets me every time I step away.
- one line: the game is pleasant to play but forgets I exist, and that is fatal for anyone who plays in interrupted bursts.
